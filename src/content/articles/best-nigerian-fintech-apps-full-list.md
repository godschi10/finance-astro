Nigeria’s fintech scene is crowded, and half the “best app” lists you’ll see are paid placements dressed up as rankings. I’ve tested the major players with real money. This is the full, honest list of the Nigerian fintech apps actually worth your time in 2026 — organized by what they’re for.

## Savings & investing

- **PiggyVest** — best daily savings app. Smooth UX, SafeLock discipline feature, instant withdrawals. Start here.

- **Risevest** — best managed US-dollar investing. $10 minimum, hands-off portfolios.

- **Trove** — best DIY brokerage. Pick your own US stocks and ETFs.

- **Cowrywise** — best all-rounder if you want savings plus mutual funds.

## Banking

- **Kuda** — best daily digital bank. Budgeting tools, card controls, smooth app.

- **Moniepoint** — best for business and POS collections. Huge agent network.

- **OPay** — best for transfers and bill payments. Fast, reliable, everywhere.

## Dollar accounts & international money

- **Grey** — best dollar account. US/UK/EU details, virtual card, 3 free withdrawals.

- **Geegpay** — best for high-volume freelancers. Unlimited withdrawals.

- **Wise** — best for sending money abroad. Mid-market rate, transparent fees.

## Crypto

- **Roqqu** — best licensed Nigerian exchange. Bank-friendly on/off ramp.

- **Quidax** — solid regulated alternative with a clean interface.

- **Trust Wallet / Phantom** — self-custody for anything serious.

## The apps I actually use daily

If you only install a few, install these: **Kuda** for daily spending, **PiggyVest** for savings, **Grey** for receiving international payments, and **Risevest** for dollar investing. Everything else is optional until your situation calls for it.

## How to evaluate any fintech app

- **Test with small money first.** Deposit the minimum, withdraw it, see how fast it lands.

- **Read the fees page, not the landing page.** Free accounts make money somewhere — know where.

- **Check withdrawal speed, not deposit speed.** Every app takes your deposit instantly; withdrawals are where the truth lives.

- **Keep your emergency fund out of apps with lock-ins.** Liquidity beats yield when life happens.

## Bottom line

You don’t need every app on this list. You need one good bank, one savings tool, one international account, and one investment platform. Pick the right four, automate them, and spend your time on making money instead of managing it.
