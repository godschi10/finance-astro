Sending money abroad from Nigeria is expensive — if you use the wrong service. Banks will happily charge you 5% plus a flat fee and take five days. I compared Wise, Grey, Chipper Cash, and bank transfer to find the cheapest way to send money out of Nigeria in 2026.

## The quick answer

**Wise is the cheapest for most people** — mid-market rate, transparent fees, and fast delivery. Grey is close behind and better if you already keep a dollar balance in-app. Chipper is cheapest for small amounts within its network. Bank transfer is the most expensive option in every scenario I tested.

## Wise — the default choice

Wise uses the real mid-market exchange rate and shows you the exact fee before you confirm. Funding with a naira card adds a small surcharge, but even then it beats the alternatives. The money usually arrives in minutes to a US, UK, or Euro account. This is what I use for rent and family support abroad.

## Grey — great if you already hold dollars

If you already have a Grey balance (from freelance income, for example), sending from that balance is cheaper than funding a fresh transfer — no card surcharge. The rate is near mid-market and delivery is fast. For people earning in dollars and spending abroad, Grey doubles as your account and your transfer tool.

## Chipper Cash — small amounts, in-network

Chipper’s real advantage is sending to other Chipper users instantly for almost nothing. If the person abroad is already on Chipper, it’s the cheapest option there is. The catch: the exchange rate is marked up, so for larger amounts the savings disappear.

## How to pay less, every time

- **Never pay with a credit card** — debit funding avoids the 1.5–2% card surcharge on most services.

- **Send on weekdays** — weekend rates are wider and transfers can sit pending.

- **Batch transfers** — sending $600 once costs less than $300 twice.

- **Compare the “total received”** — the fee line lies; the recipient’s final amount is the truth.

## Bottom line

Set up Wise, verify it once, and never touch SWIFT again. Keep Chipper for in-network instant sends. Your future self will thank you for the money you didn’t hand to the banks.
